A multiplayer drawing game where players take turns creating panels for a comic without knowing what the others are drawing. Ambulance Dispatch

Complete

Python PyCharm

An ambulance dispatch simulation that prioritizes emergency calls and finds efficient routes to their destinations. I used heaps, hash tables, and other data structures to organize the simulation data and manage dispatch priorities. I also implemented and compared Dijkstra's and Floyd–Warshall algorithms to evaluate route-finding performance and maintained a log of calls and dispatches.
